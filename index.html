Fataghoul is a student-led school initiative at Ekpaideftiria Platon that collects and recycles plastic water bottles, turning their value into donations that help provide wheelchairs for children in need. It is open to Platon students from every grade.

Where to donate See How It Works

Small actions. Big change.

Kids collect, we recycle, and the money goes to children who need wheelchairs. Bring your bottles and caps to one of our drop-off points at school.

More freedom. Brighter futures. This is who every bottle is for.

Where to donate

You can drop bottles and caps at any of these three points at school.

1

The Cafeteria

Finished your drink at lunch or break? Place the bottle and cap in the Fataghoul bin by the cafeteria.

2

The Playground

After games and sports, drop your empty bottles into the collection bin at the playground.

3

The School Entrance

Bring bottles from home and leave them at the bin at the school entrance on your way in.

Please empty and rinse bottles before dropping them off.

What we do

Fataghoul is run by Platon students. We collect, we recycle, and the money helps children get wheelchairs.

Collect

Platon students from all grades can collect used plastic water bottles and bring them to the school's Fataghoul collection points.

Recycle

Collected bottles are gathered at Ekpaideftiria Platon and directed toward responsible recycling.

Give Back

Funds raised through recycling help support wheelchairs for children who need them.

How it works

Three simple steps.

03
